Direction A
Calm with character
  • Real company logos on every card
  • Handwritten notes on the hero chart
  • One soft sage band between sections
  • Paper texture, "new!" sticker, save stamp, countdown
  • Safest for looking legit; easy to add B later
Direction B
Playful, with the bird
  • Everything in A
  • The bird: hero, save, 404, empty states, emails only
  • Bolder blocks: butter yellow, coral, sage
  • Coral dots on the chart, stickers on new cards
  • Most memorable; needs a well-drawn bird to work
